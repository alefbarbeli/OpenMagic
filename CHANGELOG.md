## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- `OpenMagicKbd` keyboard filter for the Magic Keyboard with Numeric Keypad (A1843), Bluetooth and USB:
  Fn layer with media keys, Task View, Search, Delete and navigation keys; F13–F15 as Print Screen,
  Scroll Lock and Pause; registry key maps with up to three-key combinations.
- `OpenMagicMouse` filter for the Magic Mouse 2 (A1657) over Bluetooth: one-finger vertical and
  horizontal scrolling with high-resolution wheel events, natural or traditional direction and
  adjustable speed.
- Installer with component selection, test-signing setup, certificate trust, deferred installation
  until restart, and an uninstaller.
- `OpenMagic-Keymap.ps1`, `OpenMagic-Mouse.ps1` and `Watch-Keys.ps1` configuration tools.
